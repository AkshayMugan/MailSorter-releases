MailSorter privacy policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

MailSorter is a desktop app for Windows that runs entirely on your own PC.

What MailSorter accesses

  • With your permission, MailSorter reads your Gmail through Google's Gmail API so it can sort your mail, list bills, and write a daily summary for you.
  • It only opens PDF attachments from senders you approve in its settings.
  • It can add or remove Gmail labels (for example, moving adverts to an "Adverts" label). It never deletes mail.
  • It sends email only when you set it up to forward a proof of payment, and only to the addresses you choose.

Where your data goes

  • Your mail, bills, settings and summaries are stored only on your PC.
  • Nothing is sent to the developer of MailSorter or to any other server. MailSorter has no server of its own.
  • Any AI summarising is done by a model running on your own PC.
  • Your Google sign-in token and any PDF passwords are kept in Windows Credential Manager on your PC.

Google user data

MailSorter's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to provide MailSorter's features to you on your PC, is not transferred to anyone else (except the proof-of-payment forwards you configure), and is not used for advertising.

Removing access and data

  • You can remove MailSorter's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling MailSorter lets you delete all of its data from your PC.
