MailSorter

A small Windows app that sorts your Gmail, on your own PC.

MailSorter lists your bills with amounts and due dates, files adverts away, gives you a short summary every evening, and can forward proofs of payment (POPs) to the people you choose.

Download MailSorter

Getting started

  1. Download the newest MailSorter-Setup-x.y.z.exe and run it. If Windows warns about an unknown publisher, click More info → Run anyway.
  2. Click Sign in with Google and choose your Gmail account. Google will say "Google hasn't verified this app" — this is a small private app, so click Advanced → Go to MailSorter (unsafe), tick the boxes and click Continue.
  3. In Settings → Accounts & POP recipients, add the accounts you pay and who should receive their proofs of payment.
  4. MailSorter starts in a safe trial mode that only shows what it would do. Turn it off in Settings → General when you're happy.
