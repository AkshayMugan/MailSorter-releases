MailSorter

A small Windows app that sorts your Gmail, on your own PC.

MailSorter lists your bills with amounts and due dates, files adverts away, gives you a short summary every evening, and can forward proofs of payment to the people you choose.

MailSorter is a private app shared personally by its developer with family and friends. It is not available for public download.

When you sign in, Google will say "Google hasn't verified this app". That is expected for this private app: click Advanced → Go to MailSorter (unsafe), tick the boxes and click Continue.
